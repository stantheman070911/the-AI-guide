# Part 4: Learn and Think

Using AI well in learning, thinking, creating, and living. Parts 1–3 are about shipping products with AI; this part is about working alongside it, and needs no technical background.

> **Original language:** Traditional Chinese. The chapters in this part were written in Chinese, and the English versions are translations. If the two versions disagree, the Chinese original is authoritative.

## Chapters

1. [AI Fundamentals](01-ai-fundamentals.md) — decide what to do first; understand models, context, tools, and agents.
2. [Learning with AI](02-learning-with-ai.md) — active production at the center, and research evidence for turning AI into a learning coach.
3. [Thinking with AI](03-thinking-with-ai.md) — judgment, the jagged edge of AI capability, root-cause analysis, and ways to verify AI output.
4. [Creating with AI](04-creating-with-ai.md) — a Second Brain, agent workflows, permissions and safety, and what to build.
5. [Living with AI](05-living-with-ai.md) — four dimensions of life, a skill stack, focus and rest.
6. [The Impact of AI](06-impact-of-ai.md) — what AI changes for work, new hires, startups, society, academic integrity, and data security.
