The taste calls that keep AI-assisted design and writing from looking generated. All three chapters share one idea: specific, deliberate decisions are what make work feel authored.

## Chapters

2. [Copywriting Guide](02-copywriting-guide.md) — ten classic copywriting lessons, eight additions for the AI and social era, and a pre-publish checklist.
